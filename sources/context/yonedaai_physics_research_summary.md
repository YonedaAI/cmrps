The finite-groupoid emergent-spacetime work has explored formal constructions using weighted nerve geometries. These are more concrete than a purely philosophical proposal, but their continuum convergence conditions should not be treated as established without complete proofs.

## Three levels of mathematical achievement

1. **Mathematical formulation:** Define physical structures in precise mathematical language.
2. **Rigorous theorem:** Prove that a specified mathematical construction exists or has stated properties.
3. **Physical validation:** Show that the construction describes nature through observations and testable predictions.

The research program includes mathematical formulations and proposed formal constructions. It has not established full physical validation of *Principia Physica*.

OpenAI's October 2026 announcement claims a formally verified existence result for an interacting four-dimensional quantum field theory. The announced proofs have not yet been publicly released for independent assessment, and an existence proof alone does not resolve the Yang–Mills mass-gap problem.

## Next research objective

Rather than starting over, connect existing categorical and information-geometric constructions to rigorous quantum field theory.

The central test is whether proposed foundational structures can reconstruct:

- Gauge connections and curvature.
- Quantum states and observables.
- Interacting Yang–Mills dynamics.
- Spacetime geometry and, ultimately, gravitational dynamics.

**Core question:** Can the mathematical structures proposed by YonedaAI recover a well-defined interacting physical theory without assuming at the outset the spacetime geometry they aim to explain?

A rigorous reconstruction theorem, followed by physical predictions and empirical tests, would provide a meaningful path toward validating emergent spacetime.
