# Knowledge Base — CMRPS (Classification of Mathematical Representations of Physical Systems)

**Program:** CMRPS, a 12-volume series by the YonedaAI Collaboration (PI Matthew Long).
**Built:** 2026-10-29 from the staged corpus only. **No citation in this file is invented.**
**Purpose:** structured notes per volume (claims, definitions, open questions, candidate references) for research workers drafting each paper.

## How to read this file

1. **ID spaces.** `DEF-0xx` = definitions in `docs/ontology.md`. `T-BG/T-OB/T-OP/T-QFT/T-CMRPS-nn` = theorem records in `docs/theorem-registry.md`. `CL-nn` = claims ledger (announced, NOT theorems). `DEP-nnn` = cross-volume dependency records. `[n]` = entries in the Sources list of `research/cmrps-research-map/REPORT.md` (resolved in the Literature Index below). `(Founding Document §n)` = `sources/context/classify-physical-systems/01-…`. `(YonedaAI Summary)` = `sources/context/yonedaai_physics_research_summary.md`.
2. **Claim-status vocabulary** (exactly six, `docs/theorem-registry.md` §1): `machine-checked` | `complete-informal-proof` | `conditional` | `conjecture` | `computational-evidence` | `open-problem`. Periodic-table status tokens: `PROVEN` | `CLAIMED` | `OPEN` | `IMPOSSIBLE`.
3. **The one rule that outranks everything:** no volume may carry an unproven statement as a proved dependency (`docs/research-map.md` §4 global rule). Unproven statements appear only as (a) registered conjectures, (b) hypotheses of conditional records, (c) objects of study in vol-11.
4. **Yoneda qualification, verbatim and binding on every volume (DEF-017):** "Yoneda does not, by itself, establish physical equivalence, empirical adequacy, or a classification theorem."
5. **Never conflate existence with mass gap** [23][26]. Never cite the withheld OpenAI Lean formalization as machine-checked [24].
6. **Unverified-claim marker:** an actual unverifiable claim is tagged `[UNVERIFIED]` inline. **Count of `[UNVERIFIED]` tags in this file: 0** — every claim below traces to the staged corpus. (Prose mentions of the word "UNVERIFIED" in this file are marker *definitions and instructions*, not claims. Corpus-internal statuses such as "claimed, unverified" [24][28] are *reported statuses*, not knowledge-base gaps.)

Research architecture: Relational Structure → Representation → Observation → Geometric Reconstruction → Physical Dynamics — **a proposed architecture, not a demonstrated derivation**. Output plan: visual periodic table + arXiv-style foundational paper.
- **03 (turn 3):** reads the YonedaAI Summary. Critical distinction: the six programs are **not** necessarily six fundamental mathematical families; they are research directions over potentially overlapping structures; a classification must identify underlying mathematical objects independently of the theories that study them. Three mathematical questions: Classification / Reconstruction / Completeness. Yang–Mills placement: seek a reconstruction theorem (recover gauge connections, curvature, observables, interacting dynamics under explicit assumptions) rather than assume the categorical framework reproduces YM. Deliverable: a mathematical classification table distinguishing established families, exceptional structures, equivalences/dualities/reconstructions, per-program structures, and proven/open/proposed items.
- **04 (turn 4):** timing argument (constructive QFT + formal verification + category theory + AI-assisted research converging). Hierarchy: Mathematical Structures → Admissible Physical Theories → Rigorous Constructions → Empirical Realizations — each transition needs different theorems; existence of a structure does not establish it describes a physical system, and one model does not establish every model is in a known family. Invariant record proposal ℐ(T)=(𝒮_T,𝒪_T,𝒟_T,𝒢_T,ℛ_T) = DEF-036. Formal-verification architecture: Classification + AI-Assisted Discovery + Formal Verification. Priority: rigorous classification of a *restricted class* first (finite-dim quantum systems, finite groupoid models, selected TQFT classes), then widen. Three successive goals: taxonomy → reconstruction/equivalence theorems → generation/completeness (the CFSG-analogue). Explicit preparedness: "We should be prepared to prove either that a specified class admits a complete classification or that certain natural classification objectives are impossible without additional restrictions. Both would be meaningful mathematical results."

## D. Opposing / tension pairs (drafters must present both sides)

1. **CFSG template vs physical heterogeneity** — CFSG is complete but lacks uniform parameterization [1]; physical systems have no universally accepted definition (Founding Document preamble). The analogy is *architectural*, not evidentiary (vol-01).
2. **Classification vs reconstruction** — classification is sharp only where invariants are finite/parametric; reconstruction (Gelfand/Stone/DR/Deligne) routinely recovers objects from probes [42][43][44][12][13][16]. vol-03/04/08/09 must not blur the two.
3. **Local vs global classification content** — Darboux: no local invariants [6]; Riemannian curvature: rich local invariants. Symplectic classification content is entirely global (vol-05).
4. **Classical uniqueness vs quantum parametricity** — Chentsov unique [52] vs Petz–Sudar parametric [52]. vol-09/10 may not extend uniqueness across this gap (DEP-008).
5. **Yoneda power vs Yoneda qualification** — Hom(−,P) is faithful mathematically (DEF-038); physical equivalence/empirical adequacy/classification do not follow (DEF-017). vol-04 is where this tension is *the subject*.
6. **Dimension-3 tameness vs dimension-4 wildness** — 8 Thurston geometries [60] vs unrecognizable #₉(S²×S²) [54]. The periodic table exists only in tame regimes (vol-08, vol-11).
7. **Claim density vs claim status** — constructive-QFT frontier is claim-dense (CL-01..05); the ladder discipline (announced ≠ manuscript ≠ machine-checked ≠ independently verified ≠ accepted) is what separates them (vol-07).
8. **Mathematical consistency vs empirical selection** — a structure can be consistent without describing our universe; distinct theories can give identical predictions in accessible regimes (Founding Document §7). vol-12 must keep ℳ, ℛ, ℰ separate.

## vol-01-foundations — Foundations of the Classification of Mathematical Physical Systems

**Mandate.** Establish the categorical foundations before any classification claim: define admissible physical systems, morphisms, equivalence, and composition (Founding Document §8, stage 1). The first major research problem of the entire program is stated here: **there is no universally accepted mathematical definition of every possible physical system** (Founding Document preamble). This volume defines the problem `Classify(Ob(Phys)/≃)` (DEF-039) and the conjectural target (T-CMRPS-01) without asserting either is solvable.
