## Novelty assessment

The paper is a synthesis with three kinds of contribution and no overclaiming. First, it assembles the established classification results of quantum theory into one regime table: complete classifications (finite-dimensional C*-algebras by dimension vector, amenable factors by Murray-von Neumann type and Connes invariants, symmetric tensor categories under the Doplicher-Roberts and Deligne hypotheses), complete obstructions (McDuff's theorem and the Etingof-Gelaki counterexample), and open problems (Elliott program, fusion categories, non-amenable type III strata, channel isomorphism). Second, it supplies complete in-paper proofs of the finite-dimensional layer (Theorem 3.1 and its corollaries and companions, Lemma 6.1, Theorem 6.4, Propositions 3.8 to 3.11, Theorem 6.6 in the matrix case, Propositions 6.8 and 6.10, Corollary 5.2). Third, it records two results that a classification program needs and that are usually omitted from surveys: the restricted uniqueness theorem of Proposition 3.10 and the corrected scope of the McDuff deduction in Section 5 (uncountably many isomorphism classes rule out finite lists of types; the stronger non-classifiability by standard Borel invariants is a separate theorem of descriptive set theory and is not claimed here). Novelty claims of the "first result" kind are absent by design.
