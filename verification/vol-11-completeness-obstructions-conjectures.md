# Conjectures: vol-11-completeness-obstructions

Unproved statements (as text) referred to by
`lean/Cmrps/Vol11CompletenessObstructions.lean` and
`verification/vol-11-completeness-obstructions-claim-status.md`. "Unproved" here
means: not proved in this Lean development. Statements proved in the paper with
complete informal proofs or cited to the mathematical literature are marked;
the universal classification conjecture of the series is a registered object of
study and is never used as a lemma.

- **C-1** (Borel clause of Lemma 4.3). If the isomorphism relation on a domain
  `D` is not Borel reducible to the isomorphism relation of any class of
  countable structures, then no complete invariant of `D` takes values in a
  space of countable structures (with a Borel assignment of the structure to
  the object). Proved in the paper; formalization needs standard Borel spaces
  and Borel reducibility (descriptive set theory), beyond this module.

- **C-2** (Borel clause of Theorem 5.2). If `F : A → C` is fully faithful and
  acts Borel-measurably between standard Borel object spaces, then
  classification of the isomorphism relation of `C` by countable structures
  implies the same for `A`; hence non-classifiability by countable structures
  transports from `A` to `C`. Proved in the paper (composition of Borel
  reductions); not formalized (same gap as C-1).

- **C-3** (Wild-embedding clause of Theorem 5.2). If `A` carries a wild
  embedding (a matrix-pair family with the universality property of the
  tame-wild dichotomy), then `C` carries the same wild embedding composed with
  `F`; every non-parametrization / non-classification conclusion whose
  hypothesis is such an embedding holds for the embedded family in `C`.
  Proved in the paper (full faithfulness preserves Hom-dimensions); not
  formalized (needs Hom-space dimensions and the matrix-pair universality
  property).

- **C-4** (Fully faithful ⇒ isomorphism-injective). Every fully faithful functor
  is isomorphism-injective: `F(A) ≅ F(B)` implies `A ≅ B` (Definition 5.1).
  Proved in the paper (lift the inverse along `F`); not formalized here —
  needs the categorical instantiation of `IsomorphismInjective` in Mathlib
  `CategoryTheory` terms.

- **C-5** (McDuff 1969). There are uncountably many non-isomorphic separable
  type II₁ factors. External literature (Ann. of Math. 90 (1969), 361–371);
  used as the input that instantiates `FiniteSporadicFails` and the
  uncountability clause of `ObstructionTransport` in the operator-algebra
  sector.

- **C-6** (Adyan-Rabin). Every Markov property of finitely presentable groups is
  algorithmically undecidable (Adyan 1955; Rabin 1958). External literature;
  taken as hypothesis `hmarkov` in `UndecidabilityTransport`.

- **C-7** (Etingof-Gelaki). There exist non-isomorphic finite groups whose
  representation categories are equivalent as plain tensor categories
  (IMRN 2001, no. 2, 59–77). External literature; the witness pair that makes
  `IsocategoricalEquivalenceFails` applicable.

- **C-8** (Buchweitz-Leuschke; Bekkert-Drozd; Grevstad-May). Wildness of the
  Cohen-Macaulay modules of the generic determinant in rank two (no
  finite-variety parametrization), derived-tame/derived-wild dichotomy with
  degeneration stability, and wildness of the cohomological Mackey algebra at
  odd primes. External literature; the input behind the wildness rows of the
  register and behind any instantiation of C-3.

- **C-9** (Sasyk-Tørnquist). Isomorphism of separable von Neumann algebras
  admits no classification by countable invariants (Bull. Symbolic Logic 15
  (2009), 169–183; complete proof Invent. Math. 2011). External literature;
  the Borel-reducibility input behind the countable-structure rows of the
  register.

- **C-10** (Tancer 2023; Gordon). The connected sum of nine copies of
  S² × S² is unrecognizable (homeomorphism problem undecidable); Markov
  unrecognizable four-manifolds exist. External literature (arXiv:2310.07421);
  an instance of `UndecidabilityTransport` and an input to the four-manifold
  rows.

- **C-11** (Sporadic-failure premise). To instantiate `FiniteSporadicFails` in
  the operator-algebra sector one needs, besides the McDuff lower bound (C-5),
  that no infinite family of a completed classification covers the separable
  II₁ factors — i.e. the sector is carried entirely by exceptional objects.
  The paper's proof takes this as evident from the absence of any such family;
  it is not formalized.

- **C-12** (Universal classification conjecture). Every admissible physical
  system is generated by a fixed collection of structural families under a
  fixed set of constructions, up to a justified equivalence. Registered
  conjecture of the series; its five proof obligations are four open and one
  partial (structural characterization), and its statement is conditional on
  the unfixed admissibility list (paper, Remark 2.3). Never used as a lemma.

- **C-13** (Finite-variety parametrization structure). The algebraic-family
  condition of Definition 4.2 — that the fibers over `V(k)` vary algebraically
  over a finite-dimensional `k`-variety — is not formalized in
  `FiniteVarietyParametrization`; formalizing it needs algebraic geometry of
  families (EGA-style). Until then, the Lean structure records exhaustiveness
  only, and the distinction between an algebraic family and a bare surjection
  is documentation, not type.
