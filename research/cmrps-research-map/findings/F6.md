# F6: spacetime and information geometry

## Findings

### [1] The Ryu–Takayanagi formula is a proposal, not a theorem: it is argued from AdS/CFT and verified exactly only in the AdS3/2D-CFT case, with no first-principles derivation in general dimension.
- quote: "A holographic derivation of the entanglement entropy in quantum (conformal) field theories is proposed from AdS/CFT correspondence. ... We show that our proposal perfectly reproduces the correct entanglement entropy in 2D CFT when applied to AdS_3."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0603001
- source_type: primary
- published: 2006-02-28 (v2 2006-03-07; Phys.Rev.Lett. 96:181602, 2006)
- confidence: high

### [2] Entanglement-wedge bulk reconstruction is a proved quantum-information theorem (Dong–Harlow–Wall 2016): bulk operators lying in the entanglement wedge of a boundary region A are reconstructable as CFT operators on A, strictly improving the causal wedge.
- quote: "In this Letter we prove a simple theorem in quantum information theory, which implies that bulk operators in the Anti-de Sitter / Conformal Field Theory (AdS/CFT) correspondence can be reconstructed as CFT operators in a spatial subregion A, provided that they lie in its entanglement wedge. This is an improvement on existing reconstruction methods, which have at most succeeded in the smaller causal wedge."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/1601.05416
- source_type: primary
- published: 2016-01-20 (v3 2016-07-11; Phys. Rev. Lett. 117, 021601, 2016)
- confidence: high

### [3] The holographic entropy cone is completely classified for 2, 3, and 4 boundary regions: strong subadditivity plus monogamy of mutual information give the complete set of Ryu–Takayanagi entropy inequalities (Bao–Nezami–Ooguri–Stoica–Sully–Walter 2015) — a genuine classification theorem inside holographic states.
- quote: "For 2, 3, and 4 regions, we prove that the strong subadditivity and the monogamy of mutual information give the complete set of inequalities. This is in contrast to the situation for generic quantum systems, where a complete set of entropy inequalities is not known for 4 or more regions."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/1505.07839
- source_type: primary
- published: 2015-05-28 (JHEP 09 (2015) 130)
- confidence: high

### [4] For fixed region number n the holographic entropy cone has only finitely many independent inequalities and is captured by minimal graph models, but the classification for n ≥ 5 remains open: only an infinite new family of inequalities is known there.
- quote: "We also find an infinite new family of inequalities applicable to 5 or more regions. ... We characterize this entropy cone by reducing geometries to minimal graph models that encode the possible cutting and gluing relations of minimal surfaces. We find that, for a fixed number of regions, there are only finitely many independent entropy inequalities."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/1505.07839
- source_type: primary
- published: 2015-05-28 (JHEP 09 (2015) 130)
- confidence: high

### [5] In the HaPPY holographic quantum error-correcting codes (Pastawski–Yoshida–Harlow–Preskill 2015), exactly solvable tensor-network toy models, the Ryu–Takayanagi formula and negativity of tripartite information hold exactly in many cases — proved inside the model, not for actual AdS/CFT.
- quote: "These models capture key features of entanglement in the AdS/CFT correspondence; in particular, the Ryu-Takayanagi formula and the negativity of tripartite information are obeyed exactly in many cases."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.06237
- source_type: primary
- published: 2015-03-20 (v2 2015-07-22; JHEP 06 (2015) 149)
- confidence: high

### [6] "Entanglement builds spacetime" (Van Raamsdonk 2010) is explicitly a conjectural argument, not a theorem: classical connectivity of spacetime is argued to be tied to quantum entanglement, with disentangling causing pinch-off.
- quote: "we argue that the emergence of classically connected spacetimes is intimately related to the quantum entanglement of degrees of freedom in a non-perturbative description of quantum gravity. Disentangling the degrees of freedom associated with two regions of spacetime results in these regions pulling apart and pinching off"
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/1005.3035
- source_type: primary
- published: 2010-05-17 (Gen.Rel.Grav. 42:2323-2329, 2010; v2 2025-07-07)
- confidence: high

### [7] The causal hierarchy (causal ladder) of spacetimes is a completed classification of causality conditions in Lorentzian geometry: Minguzzi–Sánchez construct the full ladder with minimal hypotheses and record the then-recently solved "folk questions on smoothability" (ESI Lect. Math. Phys., EMS 2008, pp. 299–358).
- quote: "The full causal ladder of spacetimes is constructed, and their updated main properties are developed. Old concepts and alternative definitions of each level of the ladder are revisited, with emphasis in minimum hypotheses. The implications of the recently solved ``folk questions on smoothability'', and alternative proposals (as recent isocausality), are also summarized."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0609119
- source_type: primary
- published: 2006-09-26 (v3 2008-04-24; ESI Lect. Math. Phys., Eur. Math. Soc. Publ. House, 2008)
- confidence: high

### [8] A full classification of connected holonomy groups of Lorentzian manifolds exists (Berger-list analogue in signature (1,n−1)): Galaev's survey lists all possible connected Lorentzian holonomy groups and constructs metrics realizing each, with applications to Einstein's equations, parallel/recurrent spinors, and a classification of 2-symmetric Lorentzian manifolds.
- quote: "a survey of the recent results about the classification of the connected holonomy groups of the Lorentzian manifolds is given. A simplification of the construction of the Lorentzian metrics with all possible connected holonomy groups is obtained."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.01551
- source_type: primary
- published: 2016-11-04 (Russian Mathematical Surveys 70 (2015), no. 2, 249–298)
- confidence: high

### [9] Information geometry has a sharp classification theorems landscape: Chentsov's theorem makes the Fisher information metric the unique (up to constant) metric monotone under stochastic maps on probability measures, while the quantum analogue on density matrices is NOT unique — Petz–Sudar characterize all monotone metrics by operator monotone functions ("a large class of monotone metrics").
- quote: "Chentsov studied Riemannian metrics on the set of probability measures from the point of view of decision theory. He proved that up to a constant factor the Fisher information is the only metric which is monotone under stochastic transformations. ... instead of the uniqueness in the probabilistic case, there is a large class of monotone metrics."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0102132
- source_type: primary
- published: 2001-02-27 (in "Geometry in Present Days Science", World Scientific 1999, pp. 21–34)
- confidence: high

### [10] The quantum-vs-classical uniqueness gap in information geometry is structural: Petz–Sudar give "a characterization of those metrics in terms of operator monotone functions", so the classification data is an operator monotone function, not a single metric — a proved parametric classification, unlike the CFSG-style finite list.
- quote: "A characterization of those metrics is given in terms of operator monotone functions. Several concrete metrics are constructed and analyzed, in particular, instead of the uniqueness in the probabilistic case, there is a large class of monotone metrics."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0102132
- source_type: primary
- published: 2001-02-27
- confidence: high
