# F3: gauge theory and AQFT structures

## Findings

### [1] The Doplicher–Roberts reconstruction theorem (Doplicher & Roberts, Invent. Math. 98:1 (1989) 157–218) is the AQFT analogue of a classification theorem: every symmetric tensor *-category with conjugates, direct sums, subobjects, and End(1)=C is equivalent to finite-dimensional unitary representations of a compact supergroup, unique up to isomorphism, and — unlike Tannaka–Deligne — no fiber functor is required.
- quote: "every symmetric tensor star-category with conjugates, direct sums, subobjects and endomorphism ring of the tensor unit isomorphic to the complex numbers, End(1)=C, is equivalent to the category of finite dimensional unitary linear representations of a compact supergroup, which is unique up to isomorphism... Unlike some other versions of Tannaka duality, such as Deligne's theorem on tensor categories, it does not require knowledge of the forgetful functor ('fiber functor')"
- url: https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/Doplicher-Roberts+reconstruction+theorem
- source_type: secondary
- published: page last revised 2026-05-09; primary ref 1989
- confidence: high

### [2] Müger (2006) gave a simplified self-contained proof of the DR theorem and extended it from compact groups to compact supergroups (Z/2-graded groups), as an appendix to Halvorson's Handbook of the Philosophy of Science chapter "Algebraic Quantum Field Theory" (2007), pp. 731–864, arXiv:math-ph/0602036.
- quote: "Generalization to supergroups and streamlined proof: Michael Müger: Abstract duality theory for symmetric tensor *-categories (pdf), appendix to: Hans Halvorson, Algebraic Quantum Field Theory, in Philosophy of Physics, Handbook of the Philosophy of Science (2007) 731-864 [doi:10.1016/B978-044451560-5/50011-7, arXiv:math-ph/0602036]"
- url: https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/Doplicher-Roberts+reconstruction+theorem
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2006/2007
- confidence: high

### [3] Any CFSG-style classification from tensor categories must retain symmetric structure: Etingof–Gelaki (arXiv:math/0007196, "Isocategorical groups") constructed non-isomorphic finite groups whose representation categories are equivalent as plain tensor categories.
- quote: "Beware that there are non-isomorphic finite groups whose categories of representations are equivalent considered merely as tensor categories, ignoring the symmetric monoidal structure (e.g. Etingof & Gelaki 2000)."
- url: https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/Doplicher-Roberts+reconstruction+theorem
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2000
- confidence: high

### [4] The Haag–Kastler axioms (Haag & Kastler, 1964) axiomatize QFT as an isotonous local net of C*-algebras over causally closed spacetime regions with (graded) commutation at spacelike separation and covariance under spacetime symmetries; the nLab notes the exact axiom list (including the time-slice axiom) "is in flux as the theory develops."
- quote: "The Haag–Kastler axioms (Haag-Kastler 64) ... try to capture in a mathematically precise way the notion of quantum field theory (QFT), by axiomatizing how its algebras of quantum observables should depend on spacetime regions, namely as local nets of observables... The precise details of the list of axioms is in flux as the theory develops."
- url: https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/Haag-Kastler+axioms
- source_type: secondary
- published: 1964 (original); page content fetched 2026-10-29
- confidence: high

### [5] DHR superselection theory selects admissible representations (restrictions to causal complements of ALL double cones unitarily equivalent to the vacuum); under Haag duality every DHR-admissible representation comes from a localized transportable endomorphism of the quasi-local algebra, and the DHR condition excludes long-range forces such as electromagnetism (charged sectors of QED need other methods).
- quote: "A representation π of the local algebra A is called (DHR) admissible if π|A(K^⊥) is unitarily equivalent to π_0|A(K^⊥) for all K ∈ J_0, that is, for all double cones... under the assumption of Haag duality every DHR admissible representation ρ comes from an endomorphism this way... The DHR condition excludes long range forces like electromagnetism from consideration"
- url: https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/DHR+superselection+theory
- source_type: secondary
- published: DHR theory 1969–1971; page fetched 2026-10-29
- confidence: high

### [6] Classification theorem for principal bundles: for a topological group G there is a classifying space BG with a natural bijection GBund(X)_0 ≅ π_0 Top(X,BG) between isomorphism classes of G-principal bundles on X and homotopy classes of maps X→BG; the Milnor join construction classifies G-bundles over all paracompact Hausdorff spaces (Milnor; Rudolf & Schmidt 2017, Thm. 3.5.1).
- quote: "there is a natural isomorphism G Bund(X)_0 ≃ π_0 Top(X, B G) between the set of isomorphism classes of G-principal bundles on X and the set of homotopy-classes of continuous functions X → B G... If G ∈ Grp(HausSp) is a Hausdorff topological group, then the Milnor join construction classifies topological G-principal bundles over all paracompact Hausdorff spaces."
- url: https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/classifying+space
- source_type: secondary
- published: unknown (construction classical, Milnor; reference Rudolf & Schmidt 2017)
- confidence: high

### [7] Characteristic classes of a G-bundle classified by c: X→BG are exactly the pullbacks of universal classes k ∈ H^n(BG,A) along c, giving [k(c)] ∈ H^n(X,A); the standard families are Stiefel–Whitney/Pontryagin classes (G=O), Euler classes (G=SO), Chern classes (G=U), and the Dixmier–Douady class for circle 2-group (gerbe) bundles.
- quote: "A G-principal bundle P→X is classified by some map c: X→B G. For any k ∈ H^n(B G, A) a degree n cohomology class of the classifying space, the corresponding composite map X→BG→B^n A represents a class [k(c)] ∈ H^n(X,A). This is the corresponding characteristic class of the bundle."
- url: https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/characteristic+class
- source_type: secondary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [8] The fermionic quantum anomaly is the obstruction to trivializing the determinant line bundle (or its square root, the Pfaffian line bundle in 8k+2 dimensions) of the Dirac/Fredholm operator in the bosonic field configuration space, as analyzed by Freed, "Determinants, torsion, and strings", Comm. Math. Phys. 107:3 (1986) 483–513.
- quote: "So to even start making sense of the remaining path integral over the bosonic degree of freedom, this determinant line bundle or the corresponding Pfaffian line bundle has to be trivializable. Its non-trivializability is the fermionic anomaly."
- url: https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/quantum+anomaly
- source_type: secondary
- published: primary ref 1986; page fetched 2026-10-29
- confidence: high

### [9] In the BV-BRST formulation, the gauge anomaly of a local gauge theory with resolved covariant phase space P is the pair of obstruction classes Anom_gauge = H^1(CE(P)) ⊕ H^2(CE(P)); quantization exists only when these vanish (contrasted with Kontsevich's theorem, which applies to ordinary symplectic manifolds but not derived ∞-Lie algebroids with ghosts).
- quote: "the quantization of the derived symplectic space P exists only if the first and second infinity-Lie algebroid cohomology of P vanishes... Anom_gauge = H^1(CE(P)) ⊕ H^2(CE(P)) are called the gauge anomaly of the system. Only if they vanish does the quantization of the gauge theory encoded by S exist."
- url: https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/quantum+anomaly
- source_type: secondary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [10] Casini & Magan (arXiv:2511.21810, submitted 2025-11-26, 47 pages) generalized the DHR reconstruction theorem to higher-form symmetries: DHR says standard 0-form internal symmetries in D>2 are groups and non-invertible symmetries arise as subtheories of a unique parent theory with compact group symmetry; they show Haag-duality-violation sectors for regions with non-trivial π_i (0<i<D-2) are labeled by abelian groups, with for D=4n and loop dimension k=2n-1 a Hermitian character table required, leaving open an extra Z_2 factor not realizable by Lagrangian gauge models.
- quote: "The Doplicher-Haag-Roberts (DHR) reconstruction theorem shows that standard (0-form) internal symmetries are associated to groups in relativistic quantum field theory in spacetime dimension D>2... we show that the HDV corresponding to regions with non-trivial π_i, 0<i<D-2, are associated with abelian groups... This does not rule out the possibility of an extra Z_2 factor that is not achievable by Lagrangian gauge models."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.21810
- source_type: primary
- published: 2025-11-26
- confidence: high

### [11] Müger (arXiv:math-ph/0008027; Fields Inst. Commun. 30 (2001) 297–319) recast DR reconstruction for rational chiral conformal field theory as a Galois theory for braided tensor categories: modular categories are characterized as braided tensor categories with trivial center, a double commutant theorem holds for subcategories of modular categories, and the representation category of a fixed-point net A is NOT determined by that of the extension F — extra data ("soliton representations") is needed; open problems are listed.
- quote: "it amounts to a Galois theory for braided tensor categories. We emphasize the characterization of modular categories as braided tensor categories with trivial center and we state a double commutant theorem for subcategories of modular categories... the representation category of A is not determined completely by that of F and we identify the needed additional data in terms of soliton representations... and close with some open problems."
- url: http://export.arxiv.org/api/query?search_query=all:%22Doplicher-Roberts%22&start=0&max_results=15 (entry: arXiv:math-ph/0008027)
- source_type: primary
- published: 2000-08-21 (arXiv), 2001 (journal)
- confidence: high

### [12] The braided Doplicher–Roberts program is active through 2026: Pinzari constructed a global quantum gauge group A_W(g,q), "an intrinsic construction for braided tensor C*-categories in the sense of the Doplicher-Roberts duality problem" (arXiv:2502.06229, v8 updated 2026-09-19), and states it "unifies the Doplicher-Roberts construction of a compact gauge group in higher-dimensional algebraic quantum field theory with the Huang-Lepowsky braided tensor structure and quantum gauge groups"; a companion survey (arXiv:2602.08348, v3 2026-04-06) covers the braided DR program and the Finkelberg–Kazhdan–Lusztig equivalence.
- quote: "our previous work, where the global quantum gauge group A_W(g,q), an intrinsic construction for braided tensor C*-categories in the sense of the Doplicher-Roberts duality problem, was canonically constructed... The appeal to the braid group duality, made here for the first time in this context, unifies the Doplicher-Roberts construction of a compact gauge group in higher-dimensional algebraic quantum field theory with the Huang-Lepowsky braided tensor structure and quantum gauge groups."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.06229
- source_type: primary
- published: 2025-02-10 (v1), 2026-09-19 (v8)
- confidence: high
